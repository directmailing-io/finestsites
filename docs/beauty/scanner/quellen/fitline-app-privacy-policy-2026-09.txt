FitLine App Privacy Policy
Version:
September 2026
1. Purpose of this Privacy Policy
This Privacy Policy explains how we process personal data when you use the FitLine mobile application ("FitLine App"), including its health and fitness functions and the optional Skin Analyzer.
We process personal data in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR") and other applicable data protection laws.
The FitLine App provides, among other functions:
·      user account and profile management;
·
personalized FitLine content;
·
product-related functionality;
·
health and activity integration;
·
routines and activity tracking;
·
notifications;
·
FitLine Skin Analyzer;
·
personalized skincare recommendations; and
·
optional integrations and services.
Not all functions require the same categories of personal data. Optional functions involving health information or facial-image analysis are subject to additional privacy controls described below.

PM-International

3. Personal Data We Process
Depending on the functions you use, we may process the following categories of personal data.
3.1 Account and Profile Information
This may include:
·
name and surname;
·
email address;
·
telephone number;
·
user identifier;
·
account type and status;
·
date of birth;
·
age or age range;
·
gender;
·
country;
·
language;
·
timezone;
·
profile photograph;
·      FitLine points, rewards and achievement information;
·      FitLine product information and favorites;
·
account settings and preferences; and
·      information required for authentication and account security.
3.2 Questionnaire and Personalization Information
If you complete FitLine questionnaires or personalization functions, we may process information concerning your:
·
health and wellness goals;
·
sleep and energy preferences;
·      skin type and skin condition;
·
dietary preferences;
·
sports and activity preferences;
·
product preferences; and
·      other information that you voluntarily provide through the questionnaire.
Some answers may reveal information that constitutes special categories of personal data under Article 9 GDPR. Where processing of such information requires consent, we will request your explicit consent before processing it for the relevant purpose.
3.3 Device and Technical Information
Depending on your use of the App, we may process:
·
device identifiers;
·      operating system and platform information;
·
application version;
·
authentication tokens;
·
push-notification tokens;
·
language and regional settings; and
·      technical information required for security, troubleshooting and operation of the App.
4. Health and Fitness Data
4.1 Optional Health Integration
The FitLine App can optionally connect to health and fitness functionality provided by your mobile device, including Apple Health and Google Health Connect.
Using this functionality is voluntary.
The FitLine App does not require access to your health application in order to maintain a basic FitLine account.
Where required under applicable law, we process health-related information only after obtaining your explicit consent in accordance with Article 9(2)(a) GDPR.
4.2 Health Information Accessed
The FitLine App is designed to process the following activity information:
·
step count; and
·
exercise/workout sessions.
For exercise activities, the App may distinguish between different types of exercise or sport.
The FitLine App does not use this integration to access heart rate, active-energy, blood-oxygen or similar health measurements.
4.3 How Health Information Is Processed
The App accesses relevant health information from your device and aggregates activity information before transmitting it to the FitLine backend.
For steps and walking activities, the backend receives an aggregated daily counter rather than individual step-by-step records.
Exercise information may be used to identify the relevant type of exercise or activity.
Synchronization takes place when the FitLine App is opened or resumed while the health integration is enabled. The FitLine backend does not perform continuous background synchronization.
4.4 Purpose
Health and activity information is used to provide FitLine App functionality, including:
·
displaying or tracking activity;
·
supporting FitLine routines;
·
recording progress;
·
providing activity-related information; and
·      providing relevant product or routine notifications where these functions are enabled.
We do not use health information obtained through the health integration for unrelated advertising purposes.
4.5 Legal Basis
Where the information constitutes health data within the meaning of Article 9 GDPR, the legal basis is:
Article 6(1)(a) GDPR – consent
, together with
Article 9(2)(a) GDPR – explicit consent.
You may choose not to provide this consent without losing access to unrelated functions of the FitLine App.
4.6 Withdrawal of Health Consent
You may withdraw your consent at any time.
Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before your consent was withdrawn.
After withdrawal, the FitLine App will no longer collect new health information from the connected health service.
5. Skin Analyzer
5.1 Purpose
The FitLine App provides an optional Skin Analyzer.
The Skin Analyzer allows you to take a photograph of your face and receive an automated cosmetic analysis of visible skin characteristics.
The analysis is intended exclusively to provide cosmetic skincare information and personalized FitLine skincare product recommendations.
The Skin Analyzer is not intended to:
·
identify you;
·
perform facial recognition;
·
authenticate your identity;
·
provide medical diagnoses;
·
diagnose skin diseases; or
·
replace professional medical advice.
If you have concerns about a skin condition or your health, you should seek appropriate professional medical advice.
5.2 Information Used for the Analysis
Depending on the information you have provided, the Skin Analyzer may process:
·
your facial image;
·
skin type;
·
age or age range;
·
gender;
·
country and geographical region;
·
skincare concerns;
·
skincare goals;
·
whether makeup is worn;
·
language; and
·      other Skin Analyzer questionnaire information.
Your name, email address, customer number, purchase history and previous Skin Analyzer results are not included in the AI prompt sent for an individual Skin Analyzer request.
5.3 Facial Image Processing
The Skin Analyzer currently uses a photograph captured through the FitLine App camera.
Before transmission, the App performs technical processing of the image, including resizing and compression.
Certain checks are performed locally on your device, including:
·
face detection;
·
facial landmark detection;
·
image-quality checks;
·
light-level checks; and
·
certain image suitability checks.
These functions are used to determine whether the image is suitable for Skin Analyzer processing.
The facial image is not used by FitLine for facial recognition or identification.
5.4 Skin Characteristics Analyzed
The AI analysis may assess visible cosmetic characteristics including:
·
hydration;
·
wrinkles;
·
redness;
·
dark spots;
·
under-eye appearance;
·
acne-related visible characteristics;
·
pores;
·
skin texture;
·
firmness; and
·
oiliness.
The system may also generate:
·
an estimated visual age;
·
an overall skin score;
·
textual observations;
·
skincare insights;
·
areas of concern; and
·      spatial information indicating where visible concerns appear on the facial image.
These results are generated automatically and may not always be accurate.
5.5 Legal Basis and Explicit Consent
Use of the Skin Analyzer is voluntary.
Where the processing involves special categories of personal data within the meaning of Article 9 GDPR, processing is based on:
Article 6(1)(a) GDPR – consent
, together with
Article 9(2)(a) GDPR – explicit consent.
Before your facial image is transmitted for AI analysis, you will be asked to explicitly consent to the processing.
Choosing not to consent will prevent the Skin Analyzer from performing the analysis but will not prevent you from using unrelated FitLine App functionality.
6. Artificial Intelligence Processing
We use external artificial intelligence services to analyze the facial image.
6.1 OpenAI
The primary AI service used by the Skin Analyzer is provided by OpenAI.
For analysis, a temporary secure link to the facial image is generated. OpenAI accesses the image through this temporary link together with the information necessary to perform the analysis.
The information sent for AI analysis does not include your FitLine user ID, name, email address, customer number or purchase history.
OpenAI returns structured Skin Analyzer results to the FitLine system.
OpenAI is instructed to perform cosmetic analysis only and not to provide medical diagnoses or recommend medicines.
6.2 Google Gemini
If the OpenAI service is unavailable or fails after the configured retries, Google Gemini may be used as a fallback AI service.
In this case, the image and necessary Skin Analyzer information may be transmitted to Google's Gemini service for analysis.
7. Product Recommendations
The artificial intelligence service analyzes visible skin characteristics but does
not
select FitLine products for you.
Product recommendations are generated separately by the FitLine system using predefined rules and scoring logic.
Depending on availability and applicable configuration, the recommendation process may consider factors such as:
·
Skin Analyzer scores;
·
skin characteristics;
·
age bracket;
·
gender;
·
country;
·
product availability; and
·
predefined product-selection rules.
The AI provider is explicitly instructed not to recommend or name FitLine products.
8. Automated Processing
The Skin Analyzer involves automated analysis.
The results and product recommendations are intended to provide optional cosmetic and product guidance.
They do not produce legal effects concerning you and are not intended to similarly significantly affect you within the meaning of Article 22 GDPR.
You remain free to disregard any Skin Analyzer result or product recommendation.
9. Storage of Skin Analyzer Images
For Skin Analyzer processing, your facial image is temporarily uploaded to an AWS S3 storage environment located in the EU.
The image is made available to the selected AI processor for the purpose of performing the analysis.
Following completion of the analysis, the application initiates deletion of the temporary image.
The image itself is not stored in the FitLine MongoDB database.
10. Skin Analyzer Results
The FitLine system may store the results of your Skin Analyzer analysis in association with your FitLine account.
These results may include:
·
individual skin scores;
·
estimated visual age;
·
overall score;
·
analysis summary;
·
personalized insights;
·
identified concerns;
·      facial-region coordinates associated with identified concerns; and
·
resulting product recommendations.
11. Storage and Hosting
Core FitLine backend services and personal-data storage are operated in the European Union.
The documented backend environment uses the AWS
eu-west-3 (Paris, France)
region.
FitLine uses MongoDB as its principal application data store and AWS S3 for temporary Skin Analyzer image storage.
Some service providers may process personal data outside the European Economic Area, as described below.
12. Service Providers and Recipients
We use selected service providers to operate the FitLine App.
Depending on the functionality used, these may include:
·      Amazon Web Services – hosting, storage and email infrastructure;
·      Google Firebase – authentication, cloud messaging and related services;
·      OpenAI – AI-based Skin Analyzer processing;
·      Google Gemini – fallback AI-based Skin Analyzer processing;
·      Datadog – application monitoring and diagnostics;
·      HubSpot – analytics and related functionality;
·
Firebase Crashlytics – crash reporting;
·      PM-International group companies where required to provide the service; and
·      other technical service providers necessary for secure operation of the FitLine App.
Service providers processing personal data on our behalf are subject to appropriate contractual and data-protection requirements in accordance with Article 28 GDPR where applicable.
13. International Data Transfers
Some service providers may process personal data outside the European Union or European Economic Area.
Where personal data is transferred to a country for which the European Commission has not adopted an adequacy decision, we implement appropriate safeguards in accordance with Chapter V GDPR.
Depending on the service provider and transfer, these safeguards may include the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses pursuant to Article 46 GDPR and appropriate supplementary safeguards.
Further information concerning applicable safeguards may be requested from our Data Protection Officer.
14. Data Retention
We retain personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes for which it was collected and in accordance with applicable legal requirements.
Retention periods depend on the category and purpose of the information.
Account information is generally retained while your FitLine account remains active and deleted or anonymized when it is no longer required, subject to applicable legal retention obligations.
Health and activity information is retained only for the period necessary to provide the respective FitLine functionality.
Temporary Skin Analyzer facial images are intended to be deleted following completion of the analysis.
Skin Analyzer results are retained for the period necessary to provide the Skin Analyzer history and related functionality.
Technical logs and backups may be retained for defined security, operational and disaster-recovery periods.
15. Account Deletion
You may request deletion of your FitLine account.
Deletion of the account results in deletion of the associated information from the active FitLine application database, subject to information that must be retained for legal reasons.
Residual copies may temporarily remain in system backups until the applicable backup-retention period expires.
Information held by service providers is deleted in accordance with the applicable processing agreement, provider configuration and retention rules.
16. Logging, Security and Monitoring
We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal data against accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure or access.
Measures include, as appropriate:
·      encrypted transmission using HTTPS/TLS;
·
network isolation;
·
authentication and authorization controls;
·      restricted access to production infrastructure;
·
role-based access controls;
·
AWS IAM controls;
·
protected storage;
·
security logging and monitoring;
·
backup and recovery measures; and
·      protection of system credentials and API keys.
Access to personal data is restricted to authorized personnel and service providers who require such access for legitimate operational purposes.
17. Notifications and Marketing
The FitLine App may provide:
·
technical notifications;
·
product reminders;
·
FitLine notifications; and
·
marketing communications.
Where consent is legally required for marketing communications, marketing will only be performed after obtaining the required consent.
You can withdraw marketing consent at any time without affecting other FitLine App functions.
18. Analysis of Other Persons
The Skin Analyzer is intended for analysis of the registered user's own facial image.
You must not upload or submit another person's facial image without an appropriate legal basis and, where required, that person's valid consent.
19. Your Data Protection Rights
Subject to the conditions established by applicable law, you have the following rights concerning your personal data:
Right of access – Article 15 GDPR
You may request information about whether we process your personal data and obtain a copy of relevant personal data.
Right to rectification – Article 16 GDPR
You may request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
Right to erasure – Article 17 GDPR
You may request deletion of your personal data where the applicable legal requirements are met.
Right to restriction – Article 18 GDPR
You may request restriction of processing in certain circumstances.
Right to data portability – Article 20 GDPR
Where applicable, you may receive personal data you have provided to us in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format.
Right to object – Article 21 GDPR
Where processing is based on legitimate interests, you may have the right to object to processing on grounds relating to your particular situation.
You may object to processing for direct marketing purposes at any time.
Right to withdraw consent – Article 7(3) GDPR
Where processing is based on your consent, you may withdraw your consent at any time.
Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing based on consent before its withdrawal.
Right to lodge a complaint – Article 77 GDPR
You have the right to lodge a complaint with a competent data protection supervisory authority.
You may also contact our Data Protection Officer before submitting a complaint, and we will endeavor to address your concerns.
21. How to Exercise Your Rights
Requests concerning your personal data may be submitted to:
[Privacy/DPO email]
We may request information necessary to verify your identity before responding to your request.
We will respond within the periods required by applicable data protection law.
22. Changes to this Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy where the FitLine App, its processing activities or applicable legal requirements change.
Material changes affecting the purposes of processing, categories of personal data or your choices will be communicated appropriately.
Where a change requires new consent, we will request that consent before carrying out the new processing.
