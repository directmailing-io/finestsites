Start by logging in with your Team Partner credentials
to unlock full access to all features of the FitLine App.
On your home screen, you'll find the FitLine AI Scanner.
Simply tap Start Scan to begin.
If your AI Scanner is already activated, you can continue immediately.
If not, simply purchase the FitLine AI Scanner Starter Set to unlock access.
The Starter Set equips you with the essential FitLine beauty routines,
cleanser, tonic, daycare and nightcare,
with dedicated solutions for dry skin and for normal combination and oily skin.
This means you're ready not only to analyze your customers' skin,
but also to turn their individual results into personalized product recommendations
and new sales opportunities.
Next, choose who the scan is for – yourself or someone else.
For example, your customer.
Answer a few quick questions to personalize the analysis,
including gender, age, skin type and whether makeup is currently being worn.
Now it's time for the photo.
For the most accurate result, use good natural light from the front.
Remove your glasses, keep your hair away from your face and look straight into the camera.
Position your face clearly inside the camera view and take your selfie.
Once the image has been submitted and consent confirmed, the AI analysis begins.
In just moments, more than 100 facial data points are evaluated using our patent-pending analysis matrix.
Your personalized report is then ready.
At a glance, you can see your estimated skin age, identified skin type and your overall visible skin condition.
Go deeper into the individual results and discover insights across key areas such as hydration, wrinkles, redness, pigmentation, pores, skin texture, firmness, radiance and more.
Based on the analysis and questionnaire, the app creates a personalized Fitline product routine.
Products can be purchased directly through the shop or, when scanning a customer, you can open and share a prepared shopping basket with them.
And the conversation doesn't have to end with the scan.
Save the complete report to your device or share it directly with your customer for an easy follow-up.
One scan, personalized insights, personalized recommendations and a completely new way to start the conversation about beauty with your customers.
